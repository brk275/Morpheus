# Native image previews

An explicit image viewer is implemented in the room timeline and thread replies. Native builds and its 13 focused guards passed on all four platforms at `4fd8071`; real viewer and installed acceptance remain pending.

## Behavior and privacy

- An image attachment has a native **Preview image** card/button. The image is fetched only after a click. No thumbnail or body URL is loaded automatically.
- The native viewer fits the image to its panel, never enlarging a small one past its own pixels. **Close** and **Escape** release it and return focus to the app navigation/shortcut scope. Programmatic navigation/privacy closes do not move focus.
- Implemented in source, not yet compiled or run: zoom from fit up to 8× with the buttons, the mouse wheel, double-click, or `+`, `-` and `0`, and pan by dragging or with the arrow keys while zoomed. **Previous**/**Next** (or Left/Right at fit) open the neighbouring image among the room's or open thread's already loaded messages, and show "3 of 12". Each step is another explicit request; neighbours are never prefetched and no more history is loaded. **Cancel** stops a load in progress without closing the viewer, and **Try again** reloads after a cancelled or unavailable load. **Save…** uses the existing attachment save. Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside the viewer and move through every available action in layout order: Close, Save, Previous, Next, Play/Pause, Replay, Cancel or Try again, Zoom out, Fit and Zoom in. Unavailable actions are skipped. If a focused action becomes unavailable (for example Zoom out at fit, or Next at the last image), focus moves to the next available one. The dialog's accessible description lists its keys, and the image's label gives its position and zoom.
- `media.previews_in_encrypted_rooms` remains `false` by default. Settings exposes the same persisted preference. An encrypted room, encrypted message metadata, or an encrypted attachment each require opt-in. Unknown room encryption fails closed. Disabling the preference clears/revokes any current preview; re-enabling cannot revive the old request.
- Closing, switching rooms/accounts/threads, leaving the chat page, opening sign-in, and shutdown clear the viewer. A revoked account selection stays revoked across A→B→A switching. Old results cannot replace a newer viewer.
- Source URLs and attachment keys stay inside the Matrix adapter. It resolves the actual authenticated room event, verifies that it is an image, and uses the existing bounded Matrix media endpoint/redirect policy. Text/body URLs and arbitrary external URLs are not inputs.
- Downloaded/decrypted preview bytes and pixels stay in memory. No preview file or disk media cache is created. The existing explicit **Save** attachment action is independent. It always asks for the location, starting in the user's Downloads folder, and suggests only a sanitized final name component from the sender's filename. Memory owned by the worker is zeroed where practical; clearing the image is not a guarantee that the OS, allocator or GPU has erased every copy.

## Supported inputs and limits

Static PNG up to 8 bits per channel (including indexed/grayscale expansion), and 8-bit baseline or progressive (Huffman) JPEG with one or three components are supported. Images must be at most **8 MiB**, **4096 pixels per side**, and **4,194,304 pixels**. The body limit is enforced while streaming even if the event and HTTP response omit a size.

PNG framing checks dimensions before constructing the decoder. It strips ancillary metadata without decompressing it, rejects animation, bounds palette/transparency chunks, and configures the pinned PNG decoder's 32 MiB allocation budget before header parsing. The budget applies to allocations tracked by that decoder; it is not a whole-process/RSS guarantee.

JPEG framing checks dimensions, components, sampling and the scan structure before invoking the pinned decoder. A baseline frame has exactly one full-spectrum scan. A progressive (SOF2, Huffman) frame may have up to 64 scans; each scan header is validated (a DC scan of up to every component, or an AC scan of exactly one, with approximation bits within range), and only Huffman and restart tables may come between scans. Metadata after the first scan is rejected. The decoder gets the same 64-scan cap. Progressive support is source only and has not been built or tested; the progressive coefficient buffers (up to about 24 MiB at the 4 MP limit) are outside the PNG allocation budget. Arithmetic-coded JPEG is still unsupported. EXIF, ICC, comments and other large metadata are removed before parsing. Small JFIF/Adobe color hints are retained. The decoder receives width/height and scan limits before header parsing. Before the first Exif segment is discarded, its orientation tag is read with the image crate's Exif orientation parser (one bounded IFD0 scan of that segment). The decoded pixels are then turned or flipped upright into a fresh zeroizing buffer, so camera photos are not shown sideways. This is source only and has not been built or tested. Later Exif segments are not read, and other EXIF values, PNG/WebP orientation and ICC color management are not applied.

There is one application download/decode lease, one dedicated decryption/decoder thread, at most one pending result, and one displayed image; no decoded-image cache. The network phase has a 45-second deadline. Cancellation stops publication immediately and drops its network future. An already entered decryption/codec operation cannot be recalled, so it retains the sole worker permit until it exits. Existing SDK/HTTP resolver internals retain their own implementation behavior; this is not a process-wide thread guarantee. The native image owns an additional display buffer and may allocate GPU resources.

GIF and WebP (still or animated) decode through the pinned `image` 0.25.10 codecs, whose `gif` and `webp` features this branch enables under a narrow maintainer exception for review (existing `gif`, `color_quant` and `image-webp` lock entries; no new packages). Animated PNG uses the already enabled PNG codec: a PNG that declares an animation passes the same chunk sanitizer plus one `acTL` before any image data, at most 1000 `fcTL` frames and their `fdAT` chunks; a still PNG keeps rejecting animation chunks. Implemented in source, not yet compiled or run:

- An animated GIF, WebP or PNG opens paused on its first frame unless `media.autoplay_animated_media` is on and reduced motion is off; Play/Pause (or K) and Replay (or R) control it, and turning on reduced motion pauses it. Each frame keeps its own delay; delays under 20 ms play at 100 ms. Finite loop counts end on the last frame; infinite ones loop until paused or closed, and playback pauses itself after ten minutes.
- Playback runs on the same single dedicated worker and decode permit as still previews. Frames are decoded strictly in order from the in-memory plaintext, never collected; looping and Replay decode again from the start. Only the newest picture waits for the window and at most one update is queued. Closing, opening another image, switching room or account, or revoking the selection drops the command channel and frame slot at once, and any late picture fails the same token/account/room check as still previews.
- Animations must fit 4096 pixels per side and 2 megapixels to play (larger ones show their first frame still), with at most 1000 frames per pass and the codec's own allocations capped at 64 MiB.

Progressive or multi-scan JPEG, SVG, 16-bit PNG (still or animated), audio and video previews remain unsupported. Attachments can still be saved through the existing action.

## Verification boundary

Thirteen focused regressions passed in hosted Rust suites on Apple Silicon, Intel Mac, Windows and Linux at `4fd8071`. They cover actual PNG and baseline JPEG pixels, metadata stripping, animation/size/dimension rejection, ciphertext tampering and EOF hash verification, streamed body limits, privacy combinations, permanent cancellation, worker permit ownership, queued-pixel clearing and stale view/account selection rejection. No local native build or test was used.

The expanded `media_local` encrypted/plain-image API fixture did not run in that candidate because an earlier formatting check failed. No real image-download/preview API pass follows from the unit results. Real encrypted and unencrypted attachment checks, opt-in/off reload, rapid account/room switching during load, viewer keyboard/accessibility behavior, failed/oversized images and installed-package acceptance remain required.
